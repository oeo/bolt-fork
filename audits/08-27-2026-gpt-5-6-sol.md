5. Bound account growth, peer work, discovery admission, and mempool selection cost.
6. Own startup, shutdown, integrity, backup, and abrupt-recovery lifecycles.
7. Verify routed cross-host discovery and transport outside one Docker bridge.
8. Enforce dependency, coverage, maintained-script, dormant-test, and container gates in CI.
